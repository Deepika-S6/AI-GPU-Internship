# Presentation Points

## 1. Introduction

InternGuard is an AI/ML project that detects suspicious internship offers from text messages, emails, or job posts.

## 2. Why This Project?

- Students often get fake internship messages.
- Many fake offers ask for money or promise guaranteed placement.
- A quick AI-based screening tool can reduce risk.

## 3. Dataset

- Created a small labeled dataset.
- Two classes: genuine and suspicious.
- Features include wording, role details, payment requests, urgency, and contact style.

## 4. Methodology

- Text is cleaned and converted to TF-IDF features.
- Logistic Regression classifies the text.
- Rule-based checks highlight suspicious patterns.

## 5. Output

- Genuine or suspicious prediction
- Confidence score
- Warning signals

## 6. Unique Point

The project is directly useful for students and combines ML prediction with explainable safety signals.

## 7. Future Scope

- Larger dataset
- Email domain verification
- URL scam detection
- Browser extension
- Multilingual support
